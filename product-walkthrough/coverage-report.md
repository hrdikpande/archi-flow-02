## How to read it

- **Demonstrated live**: Playwright drove the demo through the interaction and the video shows the result.
- **Demonstrated (integration simulated)**: the interaction is real in the demo, but the outside system (Microsoft SSO, email, Revit/AutoCAD plugins, file-storage watcher, accounting webhook, ML models) is simulated; the narration says so.
- **Shown on screen**: the screen or element is shown and narrated, without a dedicated interaction.
- **Explained only (exception)**: a server-side or architectural guarantee, or a not-yet-built integration, that a browser prototype cannot demonstrate; it is explained in narration or on a card and never presented as shown.
- **Not in video (exception)**: deliberately left out, with the reason.
